Privacy & Data Governance Policy

Effective & Last Updated: 2026

Your privacy is paramount to our operations. This policy governs how MHB Tech Solutions handles, protects, and stores user and client data across mhbtechsolutions.in and related internal applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill contractual obligations and provide technical services. Information gathered via project inquiry forms, demo requests, or career submissions includes:

  • Contact identification (Full Name, Business Name, Official Email Address, Phone Number).
  • System and application telemetry necessary for desktop application license validation and debugging (solely upon user consent).

2. Data Usage Principles

Collected data is used strictly for internal operational workflows, including:

  • Configuring and deploying bespoke client software and dedicated server environments.
  • Direct communication regarding technical deliverables, project milestones, and inquiries.
  • Aggregated performance analysis to optimize software latency and security profiles.

3. Data Storage, Security & Isolation

We implement industry-standard access controls, SSL/TLS encryption in transit, and restricted firewall policies on all host machines. Proprietary client databases are isolated, and sensitive customer records are never shared, sold, or distributed to third-party ad networks.

4. User Rights & Consent

Clients and users retain full rights to request verification, rectification, or complete deletion of their stored contact records at any time by contacting our engineering compliance desk.

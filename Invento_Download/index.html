Invento

Smart Inventory & Billing Software for Growing Businesses

What is Invento Billing & Inventory Software?

Invento is a billing and inventory software designed for businesses, retail shops, and store owners. It allows users to manage billing, inventory stock, customer records, staff details, and business reports using a simple and reliable offline system.

If you are searching for a billing & inventory software for Windows, Invento provides an easy-to-use solution without complexity. Download The Demo Version from the link below for free.

Key Features of Invento

  • Secure Login System – Protect business data with secure user access and staff permissions.
  • Store Dashboard – Quick overview of sales, profit, credits, debits, bills, and business activity at a glance.
  • Inventory Management – Track products, stock quantities, prices, units, categories, and availability.
  • Billing & Invoicing – Create accurate customer bills quickly with configurable billing options.
  • Staff Management – Manage staff's access to business functions.
  • Customer Management – Store customer details, purchase history, and total spending.
  • Product Returns & Exchanges – Handle refunds, exchanges, and defective returns with stock and profit adjustments.
  • Sales & Profit Tracking – Track sales, units sold, product profit, returns, credits, and debits.
  • Reports & Analytics – Analyze sales, units, profit, returns, and customers using filtered charts and tables.
  • Top Customer & Product Insights – Identify your highest-value customers, best-selling products, and most-returned products.
  • Past Sales Management – Add and search historical sales by invoice, customer, or product.
  • Ledger & Financial Entries – Maintain business credit, debit, payment party, due-date, and status records.
  • Recurring Financial Entries – Manage recurring business financial entries for regular expenses or transactions.
  • Multiple Units & Categories – Organize products using units such as piece, box, bottle, packet, kg, and liter.
  • Saved Baskets – Save customer baskets to make repeat or pending billing faster.
  • GST & Bill Customization – Configure GST details, GST percentage, and bill printing settings.
  • Offline Business Management – Continue managing your business offline with an active Pro subscription.
  • Automatic Software Updates – Keep Invento updated with the latest released version automatically.

System Requirements

To ensure the best performance for your offline billing and inventory management, your system should meet the following minimum specifications:

  • Operating System: Windows 7, 8, 10, or 11 (64-bit recommended)
  • Processor: Intel Core i3 or equivalent (2.0 GHz or higher)
  • RAM: 4GB Minimum
  • Storage: 200MB available space for installation
  • Database: Local storage support (Offline)
⚠ Important:
  • Keep Invento and all supporting files together in the same folder.
  • Create a desktop shortcut for easy access instead of moving the software.
  • Do not delete, move, rename, or modify any supporting files.
  • Deleting or modifying supporting files may result in permanent loss of your business data.
  • The Free Version requires an active internet connection to run.

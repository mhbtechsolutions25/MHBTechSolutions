What is My Voice TTS?

My Voice TTS is a desktop application engineered specifically for YouTube gaming creators, live streamers, and digital broadcasters. Powered by high-speed neural TTS, it reads YouTube Live chat messages aloud in natural, conversational Hinglish (Roman Hindi) with ultra-low latency.

Unlike standard robotic English voice readers, My Voice TTS understands Indian slang, colloquial gaming expressions, and casual Romanized Hindi. Equipped with built-in live moderation controls, contextual right-click menus, dynamic emoji speech synthesis, and custom brand watermark backdrops, My Voice TTS provides a complete companion dashboard for engaging live broadcasts.

Core Features & Streaming Capabilities

Natural Hinglish Audio

Processes Romanized Hindi (e.g., "Bhai stream kab tak chalegi?") into lifelike human audio speech.

Multi-Role Voice Separation

Assign distinct voices across 12 neural presets (Shubh, Aditya, Ritu, Priya, etc.) to the Channel Owner, Mods, and Viewers.

Live Emoji Speech Synthesis

Converts chat emojis into spoken conversational words so visual emotions are never skipped during gameplay.

Direct Moderation Dashboard

Right-click any chat user to issue instant 5-minute YouTube timeouts, permanent bans, or mute them from TTS.

Custom Brand Watermark

Upload channel logos or gaming banners to render as a faded watermark behind the active live chat log.

Direct Stream Messaging

Type messages into the in-app input bar to transmit official replies directly to YouTube Live chat without browser tabs.

Thread-Safe API Pipeline

Isolated OpenSSL socket connections eliminate bad record decryption failures during simultaneous read and write events.

Anti-Tamper Hardware Lock

Secures trial and subscription states inside encrypted Windows Registry hives to ensure license persistence across installs.
